The Press

Just the Facts

Current reporting, distilled to what can be verified. Characterization, prediction, and unattributed assertion removed. Every fact below is traceable to its source.

Edition of October 7, 2026 · Sources linked per story

Method

  1. Facts only. Dates, votes, counts, named statements, and documented events. Nothing else.
  2. Attribution preserved. Claims made by a source are labeled as claims by that source — never laundered into unattributed fact.
  3. Characterization removed. Adjectives about motive, importance, or trajectory are cut unless they are direct quotes, in which case the speaker is named.
  4. Every story links its source so any fact can be checked in context.

The Free Press · October 6, 2026

Separatist party returns to power in Quebec

  • The Parti Québécois won 59 of 127 seats in Quebec's provincial legislature, per late-Monday vote results from Élections Québec.
  • The governing Coalition Avenir Québec, in power for eight years, was defeated.
  • In the 2022 election the Parti Québécois won 3 seats.
  • In Quebec's 1995 sovereignty referendum, the separatist side lost by 54,288 votes out of nearly 4.8 million cast.
  • Party leader Paul St-Pierre Plamondon voted for sovereignty at age 18; he later worked as an Air Canada flight attendant, graduated from McGill and Oxford, practiced corporate law, and donated to the federal Liberal Party before returning to the sovereignty movement.

Vote figures via Élections Québec, cited in the original report.

Removed: characterizations of the result's significance, the author's framing of Canadian anxiety about U.S. politics, and any prediction about a future referendum.

The Free Press · October 6, 2026 · By Tony Blair

Green Party of England and Wales passes "Zionism Is Racism" motion

  • By a vote of 1,022 to 813, the party adopted a motion titled "Zionism Is Racism."
  • The motion defines Zionism as "an ethnonationalist Jewish State in historic Palestine to the exclusion and/or domination of the non-Jewish population" and directs that it be "treated as any other form of racism."
  • The motion commits the party to a single Palestinian state covering all of historic Palestine, and rejects equating anti-Zionism with antisemitism.
  • The author cites a Jewish Policy Research survey finding at least two-thirds of British Jews call themselves Zionists.

Vote tally via greenparty.org.uk, cited in the original report.

Removed: the author's moral judgments about the vote ("dangerous line," "unacceptable"), and interpretive claims about what the motion means for British Jews beyond the cited survey figure.

The Free Press · October 5, 2026 · By Martin Gurri

British officials warn of war risk with Russia

  • Sir Richard Knighton, UK chief of the defence staff, called the current period "the most dangerous period that I have known," adding that the Ukraine war shows Putin's willingness to target neighboring states, which he said threatens all of NATO including the UK.
  • Armed Forces Minister Al Carns said "the shadow of war is knocking on Europe's door."
  • Sir Richard Shirreff, former NATO deputy supreme allied commander, warned that without stronger European deterrence, Europeans face a war with Russia.
  • Jacob Rees-Mogg urged Britons to "do their duty" in case of war.
  • The BBC ran the headline: "War may be coming. Are we psychologically ready?"

Statements via Express, iNews, The London Economic, and MSN, cited in the original report.

Removed: the author's characterization of the tone as "strident, almost panicked," the "war-talk frenzy" framing, and all evaluative language about the warnings themselves.

The Free Press · October 6, 2026

Cornell students protest over 2024 assault case

  • A "Justice for Survivors" protest at Cornell featured about a dozen speakers; student Saya Taylor, 18, addressed the crowd.
  • An anonymous student ("Jane Doe") filed a September 14 lawsuit alleging seven male students gang-raped her at a fraternity house in 2024.
  • Jane Doe initially described the evening — including drinking and ketamine use — as voluntary and consensual, then requested a rape kit at a local emergency room more than two weeks later.
  • The Tompkins County district attorney declined to bring charges, finding her described participation "voluntary, conscious, and consensual," per the county's published findings.

DA findings via tompkinscountyny.gov, cited in the original report. The accused have not been charged; their names are withheld here.

Removed: the author's framing questions about #MeToo and consent (presented as open questions, not facts), and all characterizations of the campus mood.

The Free Press · October 5, 2026 · By Simone Rodan-Benzaquen

Student protests spread across France; hundreds of schools to close

  • Paris high school students began protesting over long school hours, overheated classrooms, frequent staff absences, and crumbling buildings; the movement has since spread nationally.
  • Protesters blocked school entrances with trash bins, set garbage fires, and clashed with police; some wore masks and balaclavas.
  • In Marseille, a firefighter responding to burning trash outside Saint-Exupéry high school was injured and an emergency vehicle was torched.
  • In Paris, a police lieutenant was chased, tripped, and kicked; footage included a cry of "Kill her." The report did not establish whether the attackers were students.
  • More than 100 schools damaged; 65 staff members and 170 students injured.
  • Education minister Édouard Geffray said 400–500 schools will close Monday; student unions called a nationwide protest day for Tuesday.

Incident details via CBS News, Marsactu, and Actu17, cited in the original report.

Removed: "France has been burning" / "pandemonium" framing, and any attribution of motive beyond the students' stated grievances.
