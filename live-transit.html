About this map

Real routes, real buses.

Route shapes and stop positions come from DDOT’s published GTFS schedule data; the bus markers are live vehicle positions from the agency’s own feed, refreshed every 60 seconds. This is the same live layer behind the count on the Forge Line page — the Forge Line concept itself is modeled on the transit Detroit already runs.

Route shapes & stops: DDOT GTFS. Street underlay: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Live data: DDOT via Clever Devices BusTime. SMART live feed in progress. Developers: see the transit data API.
