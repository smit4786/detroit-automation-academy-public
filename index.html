The mission

The city that put the world on wheels
trains the kids who'll run the automated world.

Detroit Automation Academy exists for one reason: the young people of this city should be the ones building the automated future, not watching it arrive from somewhere else. We teach robotics, drones, and automation the way Detroit taught the world manufacturing — hands on, no shortcuts, pride in the work.

This academy has never received institutional funding. No grant, no sponsor, no check that was promised. It was built anyway — nights, weekends, borrowed space — and it's being finished on principle. Detroit's kids shouldn't have to wait on paperwork.

Build, don't watch

Every student leaves with something they made move, fly, or think.

No pity, no jargon

We talk to young builders like builders. High bar, plain language.

Programs

Four tracks. Zero fluff.

01

Robotics Cohorts

Seasonal build cohorts — design, wire, and program robots that compete and perform. Challenge-based learning from day one.

02

Drones

Flight fundamentals, safety, and real missions. Students learn the airspace rules and the engineering behind the rotors.

03

Automation & IoT

Sensors, controllers, and the logic that connects them. The same systems thinking that runs modern factories.

04

Apple Code + Create

App design and Swift development for young creators — run with the Michigan Engineering Zone. From idea to working prototype.

The Signal

A weekly scam-watch brief for investors.

The Signal is our weekly brief documenting the scam playbooks hitting real inboxes right now — grant-list fraud, greeting-wave social engineering, romance bait, money flips — written playbook-style for an investor audience. Every edition is drawn from observed waves, not theory.

It's also how the academy gets funded. Here's the trajectory:

  1. Phase 01 — Now

    Audience

    A free weekly brief building a loyal investor readership. Reputation first, revenue later.

  2. Phase 02 — Next

    Sponsorship

    Fintech and security sponsors underwrite the brief. Their customers are the targets — their brand becomes the shield.

  3. Phase 03 — The flywheel

    Advisory

    Fraud-prevention advisory revenue flows straight back into the academy. Every scam dissected funds a student trained.
