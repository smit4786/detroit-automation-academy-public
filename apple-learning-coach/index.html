Apple Learning Coach.

Personal domain. This is Justin Smith's own Apple Learning Coach certification work — separate from Detroit Automation Academy programs and business.

Apple Learning Coach is Apple's free professional learning program for coaching educators on getting more out of Apple technology. It runs about 43.5 hours across six units — four self-paced plus two virtual workshop days — with a coaching journal per unit and a final Coaching Portfolio. It carries the ISTE Seal of Alignment.
